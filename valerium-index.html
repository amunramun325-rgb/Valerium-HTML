Notes

Valerium is a plain, fixed-supply ERC-20 — a straightforward token contract with no admin keys that can mint more of it, no fees on transfer, no hidden logic. What you see in the assay above is the entire mechanism.

The pool backing it is currently very small, funded from the deployer's own pocket rather than raised capital. That means the price is easy to move with even a modest trade, in either direction. Treat any price you see here accordingly.

Independent, experimental token. Not investment advice — do your own research before acquiring it, and be aware you can lose whatever you put in.
